See how verbs are built.

Build verbs from their parts

Turn the verb, prefix, and ending wheels and watch the word change. See how prefixes such as по-, при-, and вы- reshape meaning, and how -ся makes a verb reflexive.

Compare aspect partners

Flip a card to move between imperfective and perfective verbs: the process and the completed result. Each side shows its own prefix, stem, and ending, so the relationship stays visible.

Learn from real sentences

Study example sentences with English translations. Swipe to move through a verb's examples, and use read-aloud to hear the Russian spoken with a voice installed on your device.

Master verbs of motion

See идти and ходить, ехать and ездить, and the other motion families as stacked pairs, with an example for each verb. Add a prefix and watch both members of the family change together.

Follow a clear learning path

Explore verbs from A1 through B2 grouped into twelve practical topics. Move through the wheel from everyday actions to abstract and professional vocabulary, or choose a category and focus on what you need.

Practice in sets of ten

Choose the right Russian verb in sets of 10 sentences. Verbs you miss come back as study reminders at 9 am and 7 pm.

Course

Follow a step-by-step path through Rukarta to learn verbs with less effort.

  1. Learn the base verbs

    Each category in Rukarta has a set of base verbs. Learn these first, without prefixes.

  2. Read sentences

    Select a category and learn each verb by reading its sentences. Three verb pairs a day, so each category's base list takes a few weeks.

  3. Add prefixes

    Then learn the prefix combinations for each verb, up to 5 a day. This goes faster than the base verbs.

  4. Prepare for the quiz

    Finish by preparing for the quiz.

Questions

What is free, and what is Rukarta Pro?

Three of the twelve verb categories are free. Rukarta Pro unlocks the rest, including Motion & transport, Speech & contact, Mind & knowledge, and Force & handling.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Verb forms and example sentences are stored in the app, so studying works without a connection. You only need the internet to view, buy or restore Rukarta Pro.

Where is my study history stored?

Only on your device. The app has no accounts and sends no study data anywhere. Deleting the app removes it.

How do I restore a purchase?

Open any category with a Pro badge, tap Restore Purchases, and make sure you are signed in to the same Apple ID that made the purchase.

How do I cancel Pro?

Rukarta Pro is an auto-renewing subscription handled by Apple. Open Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions, and choose Rukarta. Deleting the app does not cancel it.

Rukarta Official Course

Follow a step-by-step path through Rukarta to learn verbs with less effort. Pick a category, follow its schedule, and finish with the quiz.

Goal
Master the core Russian verbs from A1 to B2. Know each verb’s imperfective and perfective pair, see how prefixes shift its meaning, and gain confidence with real example sentences.
Format
Work through one category at a time, from the category with the fewest verbs to the most. Each category takes three to seven weeks of study, one day at a time, and ends with the quiz. Its page lists the day-by-day schedule.
Length
About 42 weeks, roughly 10 months, in two parts, if you study every day. The exact time depends on the category.
Base verbs
Learn the category’s base verbs without prefixes: three verb pairs a day. Learn the imperfective and the perfective of each verb. For each verb, read its example sentences in the app and check the English translation. Use read-aloud to hear the Russian.
Prefixes
Add prefixes to the verbs you already know, up to 5 combinations a day. This goes faster, since the verbs are already familiar. Turn the prefix wheel to see how prefixes such as по-, при- and вы- change the meaning, and which prefixes each verb takes.
Quiz
On the last day of the category, take the quiz several times: sets of 10 sentences, where you choose the right verb each time. Review the verbs you miss between attempts. The app also brings them back as reminders at 9 am and 7 pm.
Exam
At the end of each part, take the Rukarta Russian Verbs Exam: 80 tough questions, entirely in Russian, with six choices each and only one correct answer. You read the sentence and pick the verb that fits. You see your score and the corrections at the end.
What you need
The Rukarta app, to visualize verbs and prefixes, study the sentences and take the quiz.
